**Bottom line:** one paragraph that states the single most decision-relevant
conclusion with its evidence attached.

## Executive Summary

- 5-8 bullets, each a claim plus its citation. Lead with the finding that most
  changes what the user should do next.

## Introduction

- the question, why it matters, what the deliverable covers, and what it
  excludes.
- for a report built on published practice rather than the user's own bench
  work, say so explicitly here.

## Finding 1..N

- one finding per section: assertion, mechanism, quantitative evidence with
  citations, then the practical consequence for this user's case.
- close each finding with a synthesis block:

  > **Standalone insight:** the generalizable rule this finding establishes.

## Synthesis and Insights

- recurring patterns across findings, stated as rules.

## Limitations and Caveats

- source mix, extrapolation risks, and which numbers are borrowed from other
  setups versus measured here. Never omit this section.

## Recommendations

- immediate actions in the order they should be done;
- further research that would materially change the answers;
- a short 'what not to do' list.

## Bibliography

- `[n] Source — *Title* — url`, one per ledger id, generated from the ledger.

## Methodology Appendix

- research question, scope, search strategy by theme, source selection and
  validation, analysis approach, and limitations of the method itself.
