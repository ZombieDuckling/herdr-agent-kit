> **Local overrides (from herdr-agent-bootstrap).** These win over anything below:
> - **The user's house rules come first** (`~/.herdr-agent/house-rules.md`): for example a ban on gradients, or on stock photos.
> - **Images:** prefer real assets, or generate them with the image-generation workflow (`image-generation-freely`); no real brands or people, no text inside images.
> - **A project's own brand, design system and rules come first** (its CLAUDE.md / AGENTS.md / DESIGN rules), even when this skill calls that palette a cliché.
> - **Honest copy:** no invented testimonials, logos, metrics, awards or prices.
> - **Never deploy without the user's explicit approval.**
