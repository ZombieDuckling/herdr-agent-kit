# affected-user governance checklist

Use this before citing participation as evidence that a consequential AI system
is legitimate or ready.

## scope

- [ ] The affected groups are named by role.
- [ ] The decision owner and decision rights are explicit.
- [ ] The system risk level and reversible/irreversible effects are stated.
- [ ] The process explains what is out of scope.

## representation

- [ ] Domain experts and frontline operators are included where relevant.
- [ ] Edge-case or high-risk users are represented or the gap is documented.
- [ ] Participation barriers are addressed or documented.
- [ ] Raw identities and private discussion records are protected.

## informed participation

- [ ] Participants saw the purpose, limits, and failure modes before judging.
- [ ] Data and privacy boundaries were explained.
- [ ] Participants reviewed concrete artifacts, examples, scenarios, or
      prototypes rather than only abstract claims.
- [ ] Disagreement and minority-risk signals were preserved, not averaged away.

## proposal conversion

- [ ] Discussion themes became concrete proposals.
- [ ] Each proposal names the affected role and implementation surface.
- [ ] Each proposal includes an acceptance test, eval case, or review criterion.
- [ ] Unimplementable proposals have an explicit reason, not silent omission.

## tradeoff ranking

- [ ] Proposals were ranked by feasibility.
- [ ] Proposals were ranked by affected-user impact.
- [ ] Proposals were ranked by ethics/risk reduction.
- [ ] Proposals were ranked by adoption burden and maintenance/cost.
- [ ] Reversibility and minority-risk exposure were considered.

## decision evidence

- [ ] Every proposal is accepted, rejected, deferred, or marked needs more
      evidence.
- [ ] Every decision records rationale and decision owner role.
- [ ] Accepted decisions link to a spec, issue, policy, prompt, eval, commit, or
      roadmap item.
- [ ] Rejected and deferred decisions are visible enough for later challenge.

## outcome checks

- [ ] A later review date or trigger exists.
- [ ] Outcome checks measure whether the system changed, not only attendance or
      satisfaction.
- [ ] New risks and reopened proposals have a path back into the ledger.
