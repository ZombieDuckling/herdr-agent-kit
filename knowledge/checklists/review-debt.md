# generated-output review debt checklist

Generation is cheap; review is not. Use this before accepting a large model-made
artifact.

- [ ] The requested change is small enough to review.
- [ ] The diff is visible.
- [ ] The output has tests, citations, screenshots, or another verification path.
- [ ] The human can explain the artifact after review.
- [ ] Unverified claims are marked or removed.
- [ ] The artifact does not include scaffolding, placeholders, or process notes.
- [ ] The artifact does not leak source material, secrets, or private identifiers.
- [ ] Follow-up work is recorded in a durable file, not only chat.
