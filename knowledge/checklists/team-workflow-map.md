# team/workflow map checklist

Use this before treating a second brain, company brain, or workflow map as ready
for AI routing.

- [ ] The target outcome or workflow is named.
- [ ] Entities are addressable: teams, roles, systems, inputs, outputs,
      artifacts, queues, and recurring decisions.
- [ ] Relationships are addressable: consumes, produces, reviews, approves,
      blocks, hands off to, escalates to, and updates.
- [ ] Every important node and edge has an evidence pointer.
- [ ] Confidence is explicit: high, medium, low, unknown, or contested.
- [ ] Freshness or review date is recorded for time-sensitive relationships.
- [ ] Human judgment and approval points are visible as relationships.
- [ ] Protected or secret material is referenced through a safe boundary, not
      copied into the map.
- [ ] One high-value workflow can be traced from trigger to accepted output.
- [ ] Unknown owners, missing inputs, or contested handoffs are listed as open
      questions.
- [ ] A fresh operator can answer who does what, with which data/tools, and where
      outputs go.
- [ ] Any proposed AI/automation mechanism is justified by the mapped workflow,
      not by tool preference.
