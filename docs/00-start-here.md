# Start here

**This kit turns one AI coding agent into a small team you can watch.** You talk to one agent, the
*lead*. It plans the work, starts *workers* in visible terminal tabs, checks everything they produce,
and reports back to you with proof. The tabs live in **herdr**, a terminal workspace manager made for
AI agents.

## The three-minute version

1. **Give your agent this repo's link** and say "set me up". It checks your machine, installs herdr
   and the skills (asking before anything big), and writes your *house rules* with you.
2. **Open herdr** in a terminal (`herdr`) and start your agent inside it.
3. **Say "run the demo".** The lead builds the website you are reading now, in front of you: a tab per
   worker, each building a page, the lead checking every page before it is accepted.

After that, use it for real work: "build me a site for my bakery", "audit this repo", "fix the failing
tests", "write me a researched report", "design a bracket I can 3D print".

## What you get

| Piece | What it does for you |
|---|---|
| **herdr** | One window with every agent you run, each in its own tab, with live status: working, blocked, done. |
| **The lead pattern** | One agent you talk to; it delegates, verifies and reports. You stop babysitting. |
| **Free workers** | Mechanical work goes to free or local models (OpenCode), so a big job costs little. |
| **The Fable method** | Nine skills that make any model plan, debug, verify and report like a top model does. |
| **Quality gates** | Real scanners and a running-site checker, so "done" means measured, not hoped. |
| **Image generation** | Agents make the images, icons and art they need, and verify every file. |
| **Limits and handover** | The lead watches your plan usage and hands over to a fresh or free lead before it degrades. |
| **Knowledge** | 80 hard-won laws and 35 operating lessons from running agents every day. |
| **House rules** | Your preferences (what never to do, how to report, what to ask) bind every agent. |

## The rules of the road

- **Nothing leaves your machine without your "yes".** No push, deploy, publish or email unless you
  said yes to that exact thing.
- **Everything is visible.** Workers run in tabs you can click into, not hidden in the background.
- **Everything is checked.** A worker's "done" is a claim; the lead proves it with a command.
